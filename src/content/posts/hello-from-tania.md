This is an **example post**. Once Pages CMS is connected, this is roughly what writing a new entry will feel like — except Tania gets a normal editor rather than having to edit Markdown.

## What can go in a post?

Text, headings, links and photographs. Each post can also have a cover image, a little photo gallery, tags, a place name and optional map coordinates.

When the post is saved, Pages CMS writes it to GitHub. Cloudflare sees the change, rebuilds the site, and the new entry appears automatically.
